About TripAZ

We connect curious travelers with the people who know Azerbaijan best — its people.

Our Mission

Making Azerbaijan accessible to every traveler

TripAZ was founded with a simple belief: the best way to experience any country is through the eyes of someone who grew up there. We built a marketplace that makes it effortless to find, vet, and book a licensed local guide anywhere in Azerbaijan.

From the ancient alleyways of Baku's Old City to the silk-road caravanserais of Sheki, our guides bring history, culture, and genuine hospitality to every tour.

Why We Built This

Problems We Solve

The tourism market in Azerbaijan had real pain points — for both travelers and guides. TripAZ was built to fix them.

💰

No Pricing Transparency

Tourists had no way to compare prices or understand what they were paying for. Every deal was negotiated in the dark.

⭐

No Reviews or Ratings

There was no trusted system to evaluate guides based on real traveler feedback. Word of mouth was the only option.

📣

Guides Struggle to Find Clients

Talented local guides had no platform to showcase their services and reach international tourists searching online.

📋

Opaque Qualifications & Licenses

Travelers couldn't verify whether a guide was officially licensed or qualified — trust was built on hope alone.

🔒

No Advance Paid Booking

Tourists couldn't secure a guide in advance with payment — no guarantees, no protection, no peace of mind.

✅

TripAZ Solves All of This

Verified profiles, transparent pricing, real reviews, and secure booking — all in one place.
